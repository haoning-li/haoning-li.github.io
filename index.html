I am a PhD candidate in Applied Linguistics at the School of the Arts, Queen Mary University of London, where I am in the final stage of writing up my thesis. During my PhD, I have developed a particular interest in looking into social media and video games from a social semiotic perspective, including how users interact with them. My PhD research examines how character-centric discourse is built into the interface interactivity of gacha games, which offers insight into how character progression is recontextualised within these games. I am also interested in fandom and popular culture, game translation and localisation, and broader digital media ecosystems.

Before my PhD, I completed a Master of Translation and Interpreting at Beijing International Studies University with a focus on multimodality and interactivity in game localisation and worked as a lecturer at Tangshan Vocational & Technical College, where I taught College English and was responsible for class management and student mentoring. At Queen Mary, I currently teach academic reading and writing in the Department of International Education, and I am an Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (AFHEA).
